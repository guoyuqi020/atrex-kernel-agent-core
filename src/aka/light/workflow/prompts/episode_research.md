## Direction and Experiment records

Both modules are enabled. Start with `PROGRESS.md`; open only the research files needed for the next decision. Reuse matching Directions and check duplicates before proposing. Choose executable work from the Local work queue, explicitly restart paused work, and treat the Shared direction pool as discovery-only. Absorb compatible peer ideas into a local derivative citing every source ID. Genealogy does not inherit source, evidence, status, or priority.

Starting a Direction makes it the focus. Queue reordering does not change focus. An Experiment may omit `direction_id` only when exactly one local Direction is active; otherwise name the intended Direction explicitly. Record decisive conclusions with concrete `intent`, bounded `interpretation`, and optional Record IDs or archived references. Keep measured facts in `records/`; an unmeasured Experiment is not promotion evidence. Inspect and continue imported peer history directly through `refs/atrex/shared/<trajectory>/<epoch>/head` with normal Git commands.

Close a `supported` or `refuted` Direction with matching `supporting_experiment_ids` backed by measured evidence and a fully supported Review. An unresolved `defer`, `block`, or `abandon` may omit supporting Experiments. Follow the Handoff section for report-specific Direction and Experiment state.

Claim-bearing Direction mutations and Experiment submissions return an inline independent review; `start` is not reviewed. `archive_status` says whether the claim was stored, `claim_review_quality` reports supported/partially_supported/unsupported/unreviewed, and `evidence_eligible` says whether it may support a terminal conclusion or handoff. Follow `runtime_action`; `scientific_next_check` is the smallest evidence-producing follow-up. Partial Experiments and exploratory, revised, or unresolved Direction records may be archived locally for follow-up, but cannot support a terminal conclusion, handoff, or peer publication. Unsupported submissions have no `archive_path`; reviewer outages archive the claim with `claim_review_quality=unreviewed`. Runtime returns the full Review once; afterward `claim_review_quality`, `scope_safe_interpretation`, and `scientific_next_check` remain Agent-visible. Full receipts and reviewer conversations are Supervisor-private. Revise local open records by stable ID; restart paused Directions first and continue terminal work through a derived Direction. Follow the installed Skill for exact fields.
