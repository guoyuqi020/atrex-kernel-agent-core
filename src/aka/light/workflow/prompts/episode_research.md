## Direction and Experiment records

Both modules are enabled. Read `PROGRESS.md` and only the relevant Direction, Experiment, and Gateway Record files. Reuse an unchanged Direction and check for duplicates before proposing another. A Direction may be an implementation path or an optimization-relevant probe or investigation; infrastructure work is not a Direction. Genealogy is descriptive and does not inherit source, evidence, status, or priority.

Multiple local Directions may be `in_progress`; keep that set intentional and identify the relevant Direction on each Experiment. Record each decisive result with a concrete `intent` and bounded `interpretation`; cite optional `gateway_record_ids` or archived `references` rather than copying measured facts. An unmeasured Experiment is a research note, not promotion evidence. Peer active Directions are read-only; adopt an imported peer Kernel before treating it as local evidence.

Close a Direction with matching `supporting_experiment_ids`: measured evidence is required for `supported` or `refuted`, while `unresolved` may cite research notes. Only pooled `candidate_ready` with `cross_trajectory_share` may leave the Direction active. Follow the installed Skill for exact actions and fields.

Every successful Direction or Experiment mutation returns its exact `archive_path` and an inline independent Qwen review. Only `review.accepted=true` (`verdict=supported`) is reviewed research. A `partially_supported` record is archived but must be narrowed or followed up; an `unsupported` submission is rejected before archival and must be corrected. Reviews are not projected into the workspace. Do not replay an identical durable mutation merely because review feedback or projection is unavailable. Correct an open local Direction with `action=revise`, its `direction_id`, and the complete revised claim and plan. Revise a local Experiment by resubmitting its required fields with its `experiment_id`. Runtime keeps each stable identity and creates the next reviewed revision.
