## Direction records

Direction recording is enabled; Experiment recording is disabled. Read `PROGRESS.md` and only the relevant `directions/<status>/*.json`. Reuse an unchanged hypothesis and check for duplicates before proposing another. A Direction may be an implementation path or an optimization-relevant probe or investigation; Runtime maintenance and infrastructure work are not Directions. Genealogy is descriptive and does not inherit source, evidence, status, or priority.

Multiple local Directions may be `in_progress`; keep that set intentional and update each Direction by ID. Close every local active Direction before a terminal or blocked handoff and omit `supporting_experiment_ids`. Only pooled `candidate_ready` with `handoff_reason=cross_trajectory_share` may leave Directions active. Follow the installed Skill for exact actions and fields.

Each successful mutation returns the exact `archive_path` and an inline Qwen `review`. Only `review.accepted=true` is reviewed research. A partial record remains archived for follow-up; an unsupported submission is rejected before archival. Reviews are not projected into the workspace. Do not replay an identical durable mutation. Correct an open local Direction with `action=revise`, its `direction_id`, and the complete revised claim and plan; Runtime creates the next reviewed revision without changing lifecycle status or genealogy.
