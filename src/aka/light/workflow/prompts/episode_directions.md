## Direction records

Direction recording is enabled; Experiment recording is disabled. Read `PROGRESS.md` and only the relevant `directions/<status>/*.json`. Reuse an unchanged hypothesis and check for duplicates before proposing another. A Direction may be an implementation path or an optimization-relevant probe or investigation; Runtime maintenance and infrastructure work are not Directions. Genealogy is descriptive and does not inherit source, evidence, status, or priority.

Lifecycle is explicit: `propose` creates `proposed`; call `start` to enter `in_progress`; only an `in_progress` Direction may `complete`, `abandon`, `defer`, or `block`. Restart `deferred` or `blocked` work with `start`. `completed` and `abandoned` are terminal, so continued work needs a derived Direction.

Multiple local Directions may be `in_progress`; keep that set intentional and update each Direction by ID. The most recently started active Direction is the focus; queue reordering does not change it. Choose executable work from the Local work queue; paused Directions are not next work until explicitly restarted. The Shared direction pool is discovery-only: absorb compatible peer ideas into one local derivative citing every source ID. Experiment recording is disabled, so omit `supporting_experiment_ids`; closure evidence uses `gateway_record_ids`. Follow the installed Skill for exact mutation fields and the Handoff section for report gates.

Claim-bearing `propose`, `revise`, and closure mutations return an inline independent `review`; lifecycle-only `start` is not reviewed. `archive_status`, `claim_review_quality`, and `evidence_eligible` distinguish storage from scientific support. A partial or reviewer-unavailable exploratory, revised, or unresolved record may be archived locally for follow-up, but a `supported` or `refuted` closure requires a `supported` Review under the Direction decision standard. Unsupported submissions and insufficient terminal conclusions are rejected before archival and have no `archive_path`; reviewer outages archive the claim with `claim_review_quality=unreviewed`. Partial and unreviewed records are not published to peers. Runtime returns the full Review once; afterward `claim_review_quality`, `scope_safe_interpretation`, and `scientific_next_check` remain visible, while full receipts and reviewer conversations are Supervisor-private. Follow `runtime_action`; `scientific_next_check` is the evidence follow-up. Correct a proposed or active local Direction with `action=revise`. Restart paused Directions first and continue terminal work through a derived Direction.
