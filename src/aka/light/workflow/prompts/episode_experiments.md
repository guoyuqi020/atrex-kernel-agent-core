## Experiment records

Experiment recording is enabled; Direction recording is disabled. Read only relevant `experiments/<experiment_id>/experiment.json` entries. Record each decisive measurement or research conclusion with `name`, a concrete `intent`, and a bounded `interpretation`; omit `direction_id`. `gateway_record_ids` and `references` are independent optional lists. Runtime validates cited Records and archives referenced workspace material. Keep measured facts in `records/` and source facts in Git rather than restating them. Promotion evidence requires a passing standard full Evaluate of the exact current commit.

Each successful mutation returns the exact `archive_path` and an inline Qwen `review`. Only `review.accepted=true` is reviewed research. A partial record remains archived for follow-up; an unsupported submission is rejected before archival. Reviews are not projected into the workspace. Use the review's safe interpretation and next check. Do not replay an identical durable mutation; revise an archived local Experiment by resubmitting its required fields with its `experiment_id`, which creates the next reviewed revision.
