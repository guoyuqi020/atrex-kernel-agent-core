## Experiment records

Experiment recording is enabled; Direction recording is disabled. Use `experiments/index.json` to locate entries, then read only relevant `experiments/<experiment_id>/experiment.json` files. Revise only `local_writable` Experiments; extend `peer_read_only` evidence with a new local Experiment. Record each decisive measurement or research conclusion with `name`, a concrete `intent`, and a bounded `interpretation`; omit `direction_id`. `gateway_record_ids` and `references` are independent optional lists. Runtime validates cited Records and archives referenced workspace material. Keep measured facts in `records/` and source facts in Git rather than restating them. Promotion evidence requires a passing standard full Evaluate of the exact current commit.

Each submission returns an inline independent `review`. `archive_status`, `claim_review_quality`, and `evidence_eligible` distinguish storage from scientific support; `runtime_action` describes workflow repair and `scientific_next_check` describes the evidence follow-up. Partial and unreviewed Experiments may be archived locally but cannot support handoff or peer publication; unsupported submissions are not archived and have no `archive_path`, while reviewer outages archive the claim with `claim_review_quality=unreviewed`. Runtime returns the full Review once, while the projected Experiment keeps `claim_review_quality`, `scope_safe_interpretation`, and `scientific_next_check`; full receipts and reviewer conversations are Supervisor-private. Do not replay an identical mutation; revise a local Experiment with its stable `experiment_id`.
