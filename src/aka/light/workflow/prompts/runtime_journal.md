## Handoff

Direction and Experiment recording are enabled. Follow `skills/runtime-records/SKILL.md` for the exact schema. For `candidate_ready`, select a visible Experiment with a `supported` Review under the Experiment decision standard and a passing standard full Evaluate of the exact clean HEAD; a historical Experiment may be reused when its evidence still matches. Omit direct `gateway_record_ids`. With `direction_terminal`, the Experiment must belong to a completed or abandoned visible Direction, at least one local Direction must become terminal in this Episode, and no local Direction may remain active. Pivot and blocked reports omit `selected_experiment_id`; a blocked handoff also requires every local active Direction to be blocked. Pooled `cross_trajectory_share` instead requires the selected Experiment to belong to an `in_progress` Direction and may leave work active.
