## Handoff

Experiment recording is enabled; Direction recording is disabled. Follow `skills/runtime-records/SKILL.md` for the exact report schema. `candidate_ready` selects a fully supported visible Experiment citing a passing standard full Evaluate of the exact clean HEAD; a historical Experiment may be reused when its evidence still matches. Do not add `gateway_record_ids` directly. Pivot and blocked reports omit `selected_experiment_id`; `pivot` uses `direction_terminal`, while `blocked` requires `handoff_reason=blocked` plus a concrete `blocker`. In pooled search only, `candidate_ready` may use `cross_trajectory_share`.
